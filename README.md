## Phase 1 — Grade 12 Textbook Reader Proof

The approved first milestone is a focused proof of the official-PDF reader and exact contextual interaction. See:

- [Archive audit](docs/phase-1/ARCHIVE_AUDIT.md)
- [Capability preservation matrix](docs/phase-1/CAPABILITY_PRESERVATION_MATRIX.md)

Build and test instructions will be finalized after the Android SDK/Gradle environment and target Grade 12 source PDF are available and verified.
